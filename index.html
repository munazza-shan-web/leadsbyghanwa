08 / DATA QUALITY STANDARD

Accurate means
carefully checked.

I don’t promise impossible “100% accuracy.” I use a practical verification workflow to reduce outdated, irrelevant and duplicate records before delivery.

  1. Company relevanceCheck industry, location, size, keywords and website against the agreed ICP.
  2. Current decision-makerCross-check the person’s present role using LinkedIn, company pages and available sources.
  3. Business contact reviewReview available work email and phone information and clearly label validation status.
  4. Final data clean-upStandardize fields, remove duplicates and include source or verification notes when requested.
